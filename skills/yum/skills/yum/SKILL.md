# Yum

Use relevant design evidence to improve the product, with its own content and identity.

## Controls

`[scope] [review|apply] [focus]`

Infer the target and decision from the task. Research, comparison, and review requests produce recommendations without editing. Build or refinement requests can carry those recommendations into the scoped implementation. Optional focus includes `find`, `compare`, or `adapt`.

## Pass

Identify the decision references can improve: hierarchy, density, sequence, typography, imagery, or an interaction. Use supplied evidence first. Research when it fills a real gap; a routine local correction does not need an inspiration hunt. Follow a source or browser the user explicitly chose.

- For discovery, read [sources.md](references/sources.md). Choose a small relevant search, usually one to three libraries. Load [specialist-sources.md](references/specialist-sources.md) only for a coverage gap. Include the signed-in-only on.design Popular check for visual inspiration; skip it without login attempts when access is unavailable.
- For comparing supplied or discovered references, read [teardown.md](references/teardown.md). Compare equivalent jobs and distinguish observed facts, inferred explanations, and proposed choices.
- For applying references to the target, read [adapt.md](references/adapt.md). Preserve the requested fidelity, content, components, and functional requirements.

Open an item before describing it. Search hits are leads; visual claims need inspected visuals, and motion needs a recording or live interaction. Browser tools, connectors, and supplied captures are valid evidence paths. After an access failure, use another permitted source without inventing what was behind the gate.

Keep direct item links or file identities, creator/product when known, capture age when known, and inspection date. Attach observations to their sources. Popularity and repeated patterns do not establish usability or conversion performance.

Stop research when the evidence answers the decision. In apply mode, implement the useful changes and inspect the result at relevant sizes and states. If the reference is inaccessible, continue supported work and label independent proposals honestly; do not claim fidelity to unseen material.

Return the strongest findings or implemented choices, their sources, and verification gaps. Keep the handoff proportional; no mandatory moodboard, fixed reference count, or research folder.
